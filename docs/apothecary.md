Bitter barks, scraped resins, and the things it is wiser not to eat.

What this world treats a hurt with, and what it is careful of. Canon has no healing *mechanic* -- nothing here restores anything to anybody, and the game reads `heal` as description rather than as a number. What it has is a pharmacopoeia: bitter barks, scraped resins and one mineral exudate, mostly bitter and mostly taken because somebody once found out the hard way.
