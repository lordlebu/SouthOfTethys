# Asura Chronicles — Early History Social Content Plan

**Status:** Editorial proposal only. Not a canon amendment.  
**Channels:** Instagram and X (formerly Twitter).  
**Source:** SouthOfTethys GitHub repository, reviewed 2026-11-13.

## Positioning
Recovered pre-human history, ecological intervention, military domestication and disputed records. Present the Asuras as complex historical actors in the fictional world rather than undifferentiated demons.

## Guardrails
- The Fang vs. Scale Wars are a native ecological conflict preceding the documented Asura Gondwana Intervention.
- Vaishura is ONE individual: Fang Vanguard commander, master breeder and Keeper of Forbidden Records. Preserve deceased status. The chronological order of his offices and relation of the Danava title to the Asura species classification remain unresolved.
- Do not conflate Asura, Danava, Daitya, Rakshasa and Oni.
- The historical position of the original Dwarka Gate relative to the later mapped submerged Gate remains unresolved. No fabricated coordinates.
- Do not expand the Asura-Tainted Princess, game events, participant lists or lore-only character records.
- Clearly distinguish original creative framing, fictional archival voice and source-established facts.
- Do not introduce new dates. Use native epoch labels in public copy.
- Visuals: accurate animal morphology; coherent Rajput miniature/watercolor/hand-painted technique as specified; no fake glyphs, labels, watermarks, AI texture, arbitrary grids or invented inscriptions. Typeset any captions separately in post production.

## Editorial pillars
1. The Pre-Asuran World: native Fang–Scale ecology and territorial conflict.
2. Gate and Rift: Dwarka crossing and unstable Asura Loka rift, not assumed identical.
3. Vaishura: single individual with three documented offices, known and unresolved history.
4. The Asuran Agricultural Revolution: selective breeding, draft animals and militarization.
5. Witness and Memory: Ammonite Man, Owlman, later scholarly reconstruction and its limits.

## Three proposed launch posts

### 01 — Before Demons Had Names
**Canon:** The Fang vs Scale Wars pit ancestral synapsids against emerging diapsid archosaurs on Gondwana. Gorgonopsids held fern forests; archosaurs occupied volcanic highlands and marshy deltas. A later unstabilised rift flooded the battlefield with mutagenic energy.

**Instagram carousel (6 slides):**
1. Before humanity, Gondwana already knew war.
2. The Fang: ancient synapsid predators.
3. The Scale: emerging diapsid archosaurs.
4. Competition divided forests, highlands and deltas.
5. Then an unstabilised rift opened toward Asura Loka.
6. The intervention transformed the conflict — and the land.

**Caption:** Before humanity raised a single tower, Gondwana was divided by fang and scale. Its ancient predators contested forests, highlands and marshes. Then a rift into Asura Loka disturbed the battlefield. This is the beginning of *Asura Chronicles*, a fictional deep-time history from South of Tethys. Which surviving witness would you trust?

**X lead:** The first Fang–Scale war was not simply an Asuran invasion. It began as a struggle for territory between Gondwana's native synapsids and emerging diapsids. The rift came later. 🧵

### 02 — Vaishura: Keeper of Forbidden Records
**Canon:** One Vaishura, three offices: Fang Vanguard commander, Underworld master breeder, Keeper of Forbidden Records. His office sequence is not established; his structured character status remains deceased.

**Instagram caption:** One name. Three offices. Vaishura commanded the Fang Vanguard, bred ancient synapsids for labor and warfare, and kept forbidden records. The surviving archive does not establish which office came first. That uncertainty is part of his history. #SouthOfTethys #AsuraChronicles #Worldbuilding

**X:** Vaishura was not three different Asuras. The archive identifies one commander, master breeder and Keeper of Forbidden Records. The order of these offices remains unknown. History is often defined as much by its silences as by its records.

### 03 — The Asuran Agricultural Revolution
**Canon:** Selectively bred surviving Permian synapsids; deployed draft beasts and cavalry through Dwarka Gate, intensifying warfare. Expanded food-system claims require separate editorial sourcing or clear hypothesis labelling.

**Instagram caption:** What if an ancient army's greatest weapon was domestication? Vaishura's selective breeding produced draft animals and war mounts from surviving synapsid lineages. A new logistical system could remake both armies and societies. The military breeding is recorded; the full civilian impact is a question for future histories.

**X:** The Fang Vanguard was more than a collection of monsters. Its most disruptive technology may have been domestication: turning surviving synapsid lineages into transport and cavalry. What happens to a prehistoric world when animal power becomes controllable?

## Production specification
- Instagram feed: 4:5 portrait; carousel 5–7 slides where justified.
- Instagram Reels: 9:16, 15–35 seconds as initial creative target.
- X: one incisive hook plus 3–6 thread entries for complex subjects.
- Open with a factual point grounded in canon; put interpretation in a clearly distinguished subsequent passage.
- Use no invented archival quotes purporting to be primary documents.
- Use recognizable palaeoanatomy and deliberately composed landscapes. No synthetic surface textures or faux inscriptions.
- Track: reach, saves, shares, profile visits, engaged replies and thread completion.
- Suggested recurring ending: 'Recovered from the archives of South of Tethys.'
