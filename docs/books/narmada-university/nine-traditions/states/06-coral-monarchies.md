# Vayu and Dwarkadhish: The Two Coral Crowns
*Political dossier — reconstruction for review*

**Attested anchor.** The Vayu and Dwarkadhish Coral Monarchies preserve older coastal authority and a relationship with the Dwarka Gate region. The existing celestial and Asura continuity rules remain in force: Varuṇa's title *Asura Mahat* does not turn him into a human dynastic ancestor, and the Gate does not prove an Asura homeland.

### I. Coastal lordship
Two lineages guard different reef fortresses, storm shelters and access to dangerous waters. Their legitimacy is custodial: the obligation to protect ships and restrict hazardous entry. Neither monarch can be declared the descendant of a celestial deity without established canon.

### II. Marriage without absorption
Repeated royal marriage produces the proposed **Dual Coral Crown** treaties, but the two treasuries and succession lines stay distinct. Merchant cities finance the navy and gain a representative **Coral Assembly**. The crown remains because safeguarding the Gate requires enduring, accountable authority.

### III. Machinery
Submerged mineral concessions and motorised diving provoke disputes between royal guards, merchants, religious custodians and citizens. The proposed **Coral Custodialists** argue that hazard stewardship cannot be sold as a mining licence; reformers insist on assembly review of every exclusion zone.

### IV. A monarchy within modern politics
Each monarchy can retain its own crown and national institutions while sharing maritime defence and Gate regulation. Their continuity is political, not proof that mythic agents were human monarchs.
