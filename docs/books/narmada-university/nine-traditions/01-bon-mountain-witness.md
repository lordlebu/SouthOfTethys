# Tonpa Shenrab: The Mountain Witness
*Political reconstruction for editorial review*

**Epoch: Early Age of Machinery.** Tonpa Shenrab is a mortal highland statesman and teacher. Long-established caravan routes and customary spring rights predate him. Railway surveys threaten to turn passage rights into exclusive concessions; he organizes guides, pastoral councils and charter towns into a negotiating coalition. His proposed Nine-Pass Peace separates grazing, transit and sovereignty, protects winter shelters and sets terms for rail construction. Rival khanly courts dispute his authority, but the settlement offers a basis for joint corridor law in Hyrkania and Tushara. His maps and agreements are contemporary records, not relics from Civilization Dawn.

The names are superficial inspirations from living religious traditions; these fictional humans are not claims about real sacred figures. No structured canon is changed.
