# Narmada University — The Nine Traditions in Human History

*A speculative series of fictional historical dossiers spanning Civilization Dawn to the Age of Machinery.*

This series follows the house-style of [The Chronicle of Convergence](../harappan-convergence/00-chronicle-of-convergence.md): local testimony, provisional identifications, contested transmission, incorporation into older Harappan institutions, and later mechanical-age reception. It converts the **nineteen previously discussed religiously associated names into imagined human historical figures or contested human honorifics**, rather than inventing nine unrelated new characters or treating religious deities as verified visitors to the setting.

The names and motifs are **superficial fictional inspirations**. This is not a claim that real religious figures were mortal historical people, and no in-universe society is made a direct stand-in for a living ethnolinguistic community.

## Status and restrictions
**In-universe historical fiction for review, not game canon.** The religious names should never be presented as objectively documented mortals in the real world. No new participant, genealogical tie, absolute date, map coordinate or database fact is asserted here. Later citations to these books must retain the difference between testimony, hypothesis and established events.

See also [Peoples, Names and Political Labels](../harappan-convergence/01-peoples-and-names.md) and [The Bhṛgus: Fire Houses](../harappan-convergence/06-bhrgus-and-the-fire-houses.md).
