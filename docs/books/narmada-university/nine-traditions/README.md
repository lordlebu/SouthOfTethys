# Narmada University — From Civilization Dawn to the Nations of Machinery

*An in-universe political history of how older polities became modern nations; draft scholarship, not established canon.*

## Read this series by political development, not religion
This collection corrects an earlier conception organised as nine stand-alone religiously inspired occupational biographies. **The principal subjects are kingdoms, confederations, republics, ethnic movements, dynasties, wars, markets and constitutions.** The religiously inspired historical names are one strand of the archival record, never nine automatic national founders.

The direct precedents are [The Chronicle of Convergence](../harappan-convergence/00-chronicle-of-convergence.md), [Peoples, Names and Political Labels](../harappan-convergence/01-peoples-and-names.md), and [The Bhṛgu Fire Houses](../harappan-convergence/06-bhrgus-and-the-fire-houses.md). Their independent earlier histories of Harappan settlements, the Mask Family, Kia, Vedda, Naga, Jharwa, Tuli and frontier houses remain intact.

## Claim categories
**Established setting:** names of existing nations and known actors/events explicitly present in earlier manuscripts. **Proposed reconstruction:** intervening kingdoms, named treaties, charter crises, parties, rail wars, dynastic transformations and nation-building pathways. **Unverified:** the proposed 2,200-year interval, dates, specific borders, genealogical continuity, positions of missing towns and precise ethnic homelands.

Names originating in real Bön, Meitei, Tani, Sarna, Gondi, Khasi, Garo, Toda and Kondh traditions are used as **superficial inspiration for explicitly fictional mortal figures, titles or contested memories**. The books do **not** claim real sacred beings were human historical rulers, nor assign all members of a living ethnolinguistic group to an invented nation.

**Review status:** manuscript proposal under editorial review. No structured game database, character biographies, approved geography or canonical timeline has been modified.
