# Singbonga, Marang Buru and Jaher Era: The Grove Testimonies
*Political reconstruction for editorial review*

**Epoch: Early Age of Machinery.** These are three distinct mortal political actors from separate woodland and hill communities, not one family or ethnicity. Singbonga organizes ethnic political representation through open boundary hearings; Marang Buru leads highland resistance to quarry and timber concessions; Jaher Era campaigns for protected commons, burial grounds and spring access. Industrial charcoal demand and rail surveys bring their causes into conflict with Lothal workshops and regional authorities. The proposed Grove Covenant League coordinates claims without absorbing their communities. Narmada archives competing contemporary petitions. Older grove customs predate these leaders; no meeting of all three is assumed.

The names are superficial inspirations from living religious traditions; these fictional humans are not claims about real sacred figures. No structured canon is changed.
