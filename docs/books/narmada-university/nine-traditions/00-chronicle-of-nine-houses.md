# The Nine Houses: People of the Early Age of Machinery
*Political reconstruction for editorial review*

The nineteen named figures are actual mortal individuals within the fictional setting, active around or after the proposed Year 2,200 threshold. They are not Civilization Dawn ancestors, reincarnations, gods or merely inherited titles. Their precise lifetimes remain open. The nine real-world traditions provide superficial naming and thematic inspiration only.

The preceding 2,200 years belong to their communities, institutions and unnamed dynastic predecessors. Harappan, Kia, Vedda, Naga, Tuli and other established histories remain independent. The nineteenth figures enter the record when industrialisation forces new bargains over citizenship, territory, labour and sovereignty.
