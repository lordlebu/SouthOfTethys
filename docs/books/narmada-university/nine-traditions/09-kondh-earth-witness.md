# Niyam Raja and Dharani Penu: The Watershed Pact
*Political reconstruction for editorial review*

**Epoch: Early Age of Machinery (around or after proposed Year 2,200).** All named protagonists are actual mortal individuals in the fictional setting, not ancient founders or inherited titles.

Niyam Raja is a highland monarch; Dharani Penu is an agrarian and territorial rights leader. Industrial mineral concessions threaten springs and farms. Niyam Raja negotiates sovereign extraction limits while Dharani Penu organizes cultivators for restoration and community review. Their contemporary Watershed Compact becomes a constitutional bargain with the Vijayan crown. Older watershed customs predate both.

Their names draw superficial inspiration from living religious traditions; this is not a claim about real sacred figures. Precise lifetimes and political events remain proposals.
