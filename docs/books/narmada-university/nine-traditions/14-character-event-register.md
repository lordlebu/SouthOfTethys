# The Register of Persons and Public Acts
*An inquiry of Narmada University into the nineteen living political actors of Early Machinery*

## Editorial method
Main-branch database snapshot: `7ab19eb4d27d0b755c1c2542951f858a71c3936e`. All nineteen character records are human, alive, draft, `epoch_current`, with notes placing them around or shortly after proposed Year 2,200; culture is null. No exact birth years or ethnic assignments follow. The register distinguishes recorded draft participation from recommended associations. An empty participants array is not proof of absence or attendance. Null epoch is not an assigned date. Database records are not amended by this book.

## III. Order of public acts
Only the spring-access event explicitly precedes the landslip hearing among the ten recorded acts. Other proposed causal sequences below require approval, not automatic predecessor links:

- Winter passage agreement → Nine-Pass Peace.
- Embankment repair → drainage dispute → possible guild constitutional settlement.
- Seasonal debate → instrument comparison → Twin Reckoning adoption.
- Grove legal roll and rival concession copies: order unresolved.
- Furnace compact → confederation formation → charter crisis; patent dispute may overlap.
- Spring access → landslip hearing → hill autonomy charter.
- Seed reserve negotiations → possible Seed Fleet → federal charter.
- Freight hearing and shared pasture agreement → High Pasture Treaty; hearing/agreement order unresolved.
- Quarry pact → watershed compact.

The War of the Three Estuaries explicitly precedes the Guild Settlement but neither lists these people. Do not place them in the war. Lothal Canal Compact, Rail Succession Wars and sky-island survey expansion are institutional context, not personal participation. The Dispute over Inherited Household Obligations belongs to migration-era Ila, Vivasvan and Vac, outside this register.

## IV. Further public acts worth documenting
Only if existing events cannot accommodate them, propose distinct records for the establishment of Jangubai's Workers' Houses (Jangubai), the organized Saljong Grain Movement (Saljong), and a joint petition process for grove rights (Singbonga, Marang Buru, Jaher Era separately).
