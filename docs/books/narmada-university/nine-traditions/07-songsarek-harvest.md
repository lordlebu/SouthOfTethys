# Tatara-Rabuga and Saljong: The Grain Ledger
*Political reconstruction for editorial review*

**Epoch: Early Age of Machinery (around or after proposed Year 2,200).** All named protagonists are actual mortal individuals in the fictional setting, not ancient founders or inherited titles.

Tatara-Rabuga is an eastern monarch and territorial consolidator; Saljong is an agrarian political leader. Industrial warehouses and rail exports threaten seed reserves. Tatara-Rabuga coordinates emergency supply through the Seed Fleet; Saljong organizes farmers for protected reserves and hinterland representation. Their bargaining shapes Vanga's Federal Harbour Charter. Older Tuli ports predate them.

Their names draw superficial inspiration from living religious traditions; this is not a claim about real sacred figures. Precise lifetimes and political events remain proposals.
