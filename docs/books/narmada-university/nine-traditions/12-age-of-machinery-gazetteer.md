# Gazetteer of the Age of Machinery: Eight Histories of Sovereignty
*A comparative political register, not an invented border map*

| Existing machinery-age polity | Earlier substrate | Proposed decisive conversion | Government and internal tensions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Harappan-Lothal Industrial Union | Mask Family city-rule, Harappan ports, Kia marsh councils | war-financed guild charter and contested succession | guild councils; Kia water autonomy and labour franchise |
| Narmada Academic Republic | plateau chiefs, furnace compact, scholarly charters | crown's failed ore monopoly and charter revolution | civic university republic; mining and forest cantons |
| Vanga Maritime Federation | Tuli and other eastern port communities | seed fleet and common admiralty, then federal charter | port federation; hinterland grain representation |

## International relations
No later border may be drawn from the names in this table alone. Control of locks, ports, mineral corridors and railway nodes may overlap. A Vanga ship can enter a Shwetavahana-licensed river; a Hyrkanian train can operate over a pasture whose clan council retains grazing jurisdiction. Lothal needs Narmada minerals yet competes with its certification standards. Pragjyotisha depends on eastern ocean access while resisting Vanga customs reach. The Coral Crowns' gate restrictions can constrain every shipping state.

## Map-making discipline
For every proposed cartographic polygon, require a dated source era, controlling authority, claimed versus effective jurisdiction, named treaty and evidence from the repository's geography. Until then show only **regional zones described in prose**, not precise coordinates, territorial hectares or newly invented cities.

## Names to reconcile in the sources
The possible terms **Industrial Directorate** and **Industrial Union** should not be silently treated as independent contemporary states: one possible reconciliation is that Directorate describes a limited authoritarian interval or an internal office of the Union. Likewise **Vanga Maritime Thalassocracy** can describe maritime dominance while **Federation** denotes constitutional form. These reconciliations remain proposals, subject to verification against canonical records.
