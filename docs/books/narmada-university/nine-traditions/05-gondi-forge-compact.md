# Bada Deo, Kupar Lingo and Jangubai: The Forge Compact
*Political reconstruction for editorial review*

**Epoch: Early Age of Machinery (around or after proposed Year 2,200).** All named protagonists are actual mortal individuals in the fictional setting, not ancient founders or inherited titles.

Bada Deo is a confederation founder organizing mining councils against ore monopolies. Kupar Lingo is a cultural nationalist and craft-law reformer promoting public instruction and local languages. Jangubai leads workers against debt bondage and unsafe lodgings. Their contemporary Forge Compact draws on older furnace customs; their constituencies shape the Plateau Charter Crisis and Narmada Academic Republic.

Their names draw superficial inspiration from living religious traditions; this is not a claim about real sacred figures. Precise lifetimes and political events remain proposals.
