# Aryaman: Testimony and Later Inheritance

*Studies in Ancient Testimony — Houses of Passage*  
*Narmada University, Collegium of Historical Inquiry*

## I. The Name and Its Witnesses
**Working classification:** hospitality negotiator and ritual convener. The University's use of this name is not proof of a single historical person. Where an office, collective or celestial entity is possible, those possibilities remain distinct.

## II. Historical Appearance and Disposition
Lean, dark-eyed middle-aged negotiator with a weathered jaw and a travel-worn wrap; courteous but uncompromising about public consent. This is an illustrative historical portrait, not an excavated likeness. Distinct communities may have pictured the bearer differently.

## III. The Earlier Tradition
The tradition is not assigned a shared original homeland with other ancestral names. Accounts that place all figures in one founding council are late attempts to reconcile traditions that travelled separately. The earliest dates, migration routes and original witnesses remain unresolved.

## IV. The Post-Arrival Problem
With Wiznu attempted to invoke celestial Indr; no appearance is established. The dispute matters because later institutions could inherit the practice while disagreeing about its moral meaning, supernatural efficacy or right of custody. A rite's survival is not evidence that its original explanation was correct.

## V. Later Peoples and Institutions
**Communities for investigation:** Jharwa alliances, crossing custodians and Wiznu's assemblies. **Characteristic inheritance:** protected passage, consent and witnessed hospitality. These are proposed transmission paths rather than established exclusive tribal cults. Naga communities are considered as historical custodians where testimony supports that association.

## VI. Competing Testimonies
A custodian's account treats the practice as a genuine inheritance; a neighbouring account may describe borrowing, appropriation or independent discovery. The University records both without manufacturing blood descent. Connections with the Jharwa, Vedda, Naga and Harappan histories require corroboration in this setting rather than analogy to other traditions.

## The attempted invocation
Aryaman gathered consent and witnesses when he and Wiznu attempted to invoke Indr. This does not prove that Indr appeared or intervened. Vaiśvānara's fire custodians are associated with the account, but the participants' exact lifetimes and the location are unresolved.

## VII. Closing Judgment and Evidence Register
**Existing canon:** Broad migration sequence, distinct later populations and already-established celestial identities where applicable. **Proposed:** Portraits of mortal bearers, later custodians, practice-specialization and specific disputes. **Unresolved:** original homeland, chronology, ancestry, actual ritual efficacy and the identity of historical officeholders. No character JSON, event, map, place, game or other structured canon is changed.
