# Mitra: Witness, Office and Later Transmission

*Studies in Ancient Testimony — Celestial Appeals and Boundary Traditions*  
*Narmada University — Collegium of Historical Inquiry*

## I. The Name and Its Witnesses
**Classification:** existing celestial oath-binder. A shared name does not import any outside mythology, and an inherited office does not automatically identify a single founder.

## II. Portrait and Disposition
No mortal physical portrait is proposed: a celestial identity is not a human migrant. Later visual descriptions are religious testimonies, not verified anatomy. Their reputation reflects communities trying to explain a difficult practice, not an attested biography.

## III. The Problem Remembered
The celestial Mitra is not silently equated with mortal Mithra. Rival communities preserve different explanations for the same practice; a later attribution is not proof of the original actor's presence.

## IV. Later Custodians
**Proposed communities:** Jharwa treaties, Harappan alliances and witness traditions. **Distinctive inheritance:** binding agreements and reconciliation. These links describe possible social transmission, not exclusive tribal worship, biological descent, or serpent iconography for Nagas.

## V. Transmission After Arrival
Distinct migrating populations carried their own practices. They became interwoven after arrival on Jambhudweepa, and some communities later attached celestial names to older techniques. Where the identity is already celestial, this is a history of **veneration and interpretation**, not the life of a mortal namesake.

## VI. Canon and Contested Memory
Keep celestial Mitra separate from mortal Mithra and mortal-shell namesakes. Similar names are not evidence of identical persons.

The University's claims remain subject to disagreement between custodians, archaeological witnesses and oral accounts. Pashupati's older court predates the major Jharwa migration; no new direct ancestry or founding role is asserted.

## VII. Closing Judgment
**Existing canon:** established celestial identities where noted, South of Tethys populations and broad migration chronology. **Proposed:** specific custodians, rituals and disputes. **Unresolved:** earliest witnesses, sites, chronology and efficacy. No structured canon or game content is altered.
