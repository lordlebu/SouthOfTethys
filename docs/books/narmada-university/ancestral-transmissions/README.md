## Continuity rules
- Existing celestial Indr, Varuṇa, Mitra and Dyauṣ retain their identities; no mortal replacements.
- Wiznu and Aryaman attempted to invoke Indr, without a verified manifestation.
- Vaiśvānara's fire observances do not overwrite Indr's canonical association with Agun.
- The extant Alms Step temple remains the later Jharwa memorial, not a building credited to Wiznu and Aryaman.
- Pashupati's pre-Jharwa court predates major Jharwa migration. Ritual fragments may survive, without invented direct descent.
- No changes outside `docs/books/`; no character JSON, events, maps or game changes.
