# Character–Event Concordance: The Asura Histories

*Narmada University, Collegium of Historical Inquiry — critical registry companion to Volumes I–VIII*  
*Audit basis: `database/characters/*.json`, relevant `database/events/*.json`, `database/species.json`, and `database/timeline/epochs.json` as inspected on the repository's main branch, 24 November 2026.*

## How this register should be read

This is the **single cross-reference** between the eight books and the structured records, not a ninth narrative volume. A **direct participant** occurs in an event's `participants` array; a **witness** or **thematic association** may be attested in prose but absent from that array. A predecessor or successor edge orders events but does not transfer all its participants to neighbouring events. The `epoch` on a character is intended to indicate **first appearance**, not every era during which the character can act. `epoch: null` is permitted by the event and character schemas; it must not be silently assigned a date.

The registry's `species: "asura"` is explicitly a **broad category** in [species.json](../../../database/species.json), used also for named Rakshasa, Oni and Daitya lineages. It **does not establish genetic or ancestral Asura descent**. Sarishne remains Rakshasa, Gagdji Oni, Kalyani Daitya. Vusajc's species remains unknown. Titles such as Vaishura's *Danava Keeper* likewise do not create a second individual.

**Scope:** Shukradeva, Daedrasura, Vaishura, Manjalaya (Vishaili), Kalyani, Sarishne, Gagdji and Vusajc Karthakur only. Other names appear solely as supporting participants or witnesses to their associated events.

## Reconciliation findings — do not silently amend structured canon

1. **Shukradeva: missing standalone character record.** The prehistoric Seedbearer event names him in its summary and causes but does not list him as a participant ID. Kunti's primary character record corroborates his role. Do not invent a character ID or assign a character epoch without approval.

1. **Daedrasura: chronologically inconsistent first appearance.** `character_daedrasura.epoch = epoch_civilization_dawn`, yet `event_kunti_against_jambanson.epoch = epoch_prehistoric` lists Daedrasura as a participant. The event is **secondary** and may preserve an older tradition, a name reused, or incorrect periodization; none of those explanations is proven. Volume III marks the dispute without adding an epic character to its storyline.
1. **Shadow Pact: site distinction remains unresolved.** `event_shadow_pact.location = place_ancient_courts` while its summary specifies a Dwarka Gate. A later migration-era gate episode has `location: null`. Neither an identical gate nor a fixed Deep Antiquity coordinate is established.
1. **Manjalaya's death: deliberately undated.** `event_last_attendance_of_manjalaya` is an existing **draft** with `epoch: null` and `location: null`. It is a permissible, explicit unknown, not a missing date that should be guessed. The character retains `status: mythic`.
1. **Taxonomy: broad index is not genealogy.** The `species: "asura"` entries for Sarishne, Gagdji and Kalyani reflect the declared broad schema vocabulary. Their Rakshasa/Oni/Daitya identities remain distinct, with no blood descent claimed.
1. **Participant versus witness.** Sarishne was deliberately *not* put on `event_shadow_pact.participants` because her witness account is concealed. Daedrasura likewise should not be appended to the Tendua Crisis or blood-feud event merely because related records describe his influence.
1. **No attested event to attach.** Kalyani and Vusajc both have structured draft character records but no directly associated indexed event discovered in this audit. Their unknown epochs stay unknown.

## Editorial and migration boundaries

The eight books can discuss documentary contradictions; they **must not** create a second Vaishura, assign epochs to undated witnesses, invent participation, relabel Varuṇa, infer an Asura homeland, or modify the Asura-Tainted Princess's protected storyline. This concordance neither updates `database/` nor replaces the event graph. Earlier continuity prose in `docs/asura_approved_character_dossiers.md` predates the current draft structured records and still describes several characters as lore-only; that separate legacy document is **not** edited here.

**Source discipline:** a recorded participant is evidence of an event-file assertion, not independently verified history. A secondary or draft record cannot overrule a primary chronology merely because its JSON field is populated.
