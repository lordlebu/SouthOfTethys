# Scholarly Concordance of the Early Songs
*Comparative source ledger for the Harappan Convergence Chronicles*

This register is maintained separately from the narrative chapters so that the chronicles may be read as a continuous history. It gives the principal comparative textual anchors, classifies what they actually support, and records where the University is making an additional reconstruction.

## Methodological notes

1. **Poem versus index.** A seer's name assigned by a traditional hymn index is not identical in evidentiary status to a name present in the metrical verse.
2. **Victory versus census.** The Battle of Ten Kings is narrated through praise and political rhetoric. Its list does not straightforwardly yield a ten-nation geopolitical map.
3. **Narrative versus biography.** Viśvāmitra's royal-to-ṛṣi life is the chronicle's adopted biographical tradition. The early hymns support his status as seer and the river-passage account, not every stage of renunciation.
4. **Title versus people.** Dasyu is not assigned a self-identifying nation. Ahi is not treated as a human ethnicity; Paṇi may be an adversarial description.
5. **Historical archives.** The University compares these texts against the independent chronological and political records of Jambhudweepa. An attractive parallel cannot replace a missing inscription.
6. **Divine testimony.** Indr remains an existing celestial being. A battle hymn praising his aid remains a partisan historical witness; divine identity and human military reporting are different questions.

## Unverified or later elements
- A documented Rigvedic account of Viśvāmitra relinquishing a royal throne: **not established** by the cited hymns.
- An identification of the Vedda Kashyapa with a particular hymn composer: **not established**.
- A South of Tethys location for the Paruṣṇī or the Vipāś–Śutudrī crossing: **not established**.
- The distinction between **Parni** and **Paṇi**: **unresolved** pending orthographic evidence.
- Any direct genealogy from the named coalition peoples to the Harappan-Lothal Industrial Union: **not established**.

The concordance is a working ledger; corrections should be entered here before they are echoed across chapters.
