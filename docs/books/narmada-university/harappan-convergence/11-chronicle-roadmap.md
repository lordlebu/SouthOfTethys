# Editorial Roadmap: From the Later Migrations to the Age of Machinery
*Work programme of the Narmada University historical commission*

## Project objective
Develop a traceable history of how distinct migrant peoples, established Vedda and Naga communities, independent rulers and Harappan city institutions interacted from the late Era of Human Migrations through Civilization Dawn into the Age of Machinery.

**A finished volume must distinguish contact, political alliance, civic incorporation, institutional borrowing, coerced displacement and proven descent.** None of those categories is interchangeable.

## Phase I — Record the people before locating their descendants
- [x] Establish a provisional register for Anu, Druhyu, Pūru, Bharata, Tṛtsu, Yadu, Turvaśa, Bhṛgu, Paṇi, Dasyu and Ahi.
- [x] Separate peoples, offices, lineages, adversarial labels and beings.
- [x] Preserve Vedda Kashyapa's existing oral-historical place.
- [x] Record the Naga courts as independent participants.
- [ ] Verify variant names, including Parni/Paṇi, against the comparative corpus.
- [ ] Identify self-designations wherever possible; do not publish rivals' insults as an ethnic census.
- [ ] Commission independent locality dossiers for Paktha, Bhalāna, Alina, Śiva, Viṣāṇin and other conflict names before placing any on the atlas.

**Gate:** No name receives a fixed homeland, biological lineage or royal succession without at least a second independent local witness.

## Phase II — Create the royal and scholastic biographies
- [x] Draft Divodāsa as an earlier ruler.
- [x] Draft Sudās and the coalition conflict.
- [x] Draft Viśvāmitra's transition from rājā to ṛṣi.
- [x] Draft the divided Bhṛgu fire schools.
- [x] Draft Nārada and the travelling witness tradition.
- [ ] Write a dedicated Vasiṣṭha chapter with the victorious court's viewpoint and its evidentiary limits.
- [ ] Confirm the relation of Sudās, Divodāsa, the Bharatas and the Tṛtsus without inserting unnamed invented generations.
- [ ] Locate a local source for each key turning point in the Viśvāmitra biography, or mark the scene as university literary reconstruction.

**Gate:** Rigvedic passages, seer attributions and adopted biographical narratives must be catalogued separately.

## Phase III — Reconstruct the war and its immediate consequences
- [x] Draft the multi-party war chapter.
- [ ] Make a name-by-name order of battle with uncertainty and competing readings.
- [ ] Gather a non-victor account if the world archive contains one.
- [ ] Investigate river crossings and border disputes without assigning unsupported map coordinates.
- [ ] Distinguish the fate of leaders, armies, households and ordinary residents after the war.
- [ ] Record which defeated groups remained independent.

**Gate:** The title 'Ten Kings' must not become a list of ten falsely precise modern nation-states.

## Phase IV — Reconcile with established Civilization Dawn
- [x] Draft mechanisms of market, ferry, workshop, council and sanctuary integration.
- [ ] Check every proposed connection against the Founding of Lothal, Stone Pact, Mask Family, Kia alliance, Black Lotus Siege and Tendua Crisis.
- [ ] Build a ledger for each claimed Harappan ward, guild and ritual school: earliest record, receiving institution, witness and date.
- [ ] Audit contacts between Harappan merchants, Vedda communities and Naga courts.
- [ ] Identify independent craft contributions that cannot be assigned to the migrant houses.

**Gate:** No older event is moved, renamed or given a new cause to fit the arrival of a newly added people.

## Phase V — Trace the industrial inheritance
- [x] Draft the industrial-successor chapter using the pre-Shattering national register.
- [ ] Test possible continuity from caravan rights to rail charters, ferry dues to canal law, hearth practice to workshop standards, oral witness to ledger or patent.
- [ ] Examine the Harappan-Lothal Industrial Union as a documented composite of Harappan, Mask Family and urbanised Kia traditions.
- [ ] Track related—but independent—developments in the Narmada Academic Republic, Shwetavahana Riverine League, Vanga Federation and northern Rail-Khanates.
- [ ] Determine whether the Kingdom of Pragjyotisha retained particular Harappan records or merely the polity's remnant status.
- [ ] Leave Prince Vijaya's realm without a presumed ethnic descent until the world archive establishes one.
- [ ] Close the period before the Great Shattering; leave cataclysm causes to their established chronology.

**Gate:** Each alleged ancient-to-industrial continuity must be supported by an intermediate institution or explicitly remain a hypothesis.

## Phase VI — Commission, review and publication
- [ ] Review spelling and transliteration; select consistent public-facing house names.
- [ ] Give every new historical assertion a provenance class: structured archive, narrated in-world testimony, comparative source, or scholarly reconstruction.
- [ ] Review individual chapters for chronology, independent populations and contradictions.
- [ ] Confirm maps, dates and political borders before proposing any structured canon.
- [ ] Prepare a second review of proposed institutional inheritances before integrating them into the published world history.
- [ ] If approved, propose **separate** structured-canon additions in a later change set; do not silently alter entity JSON from narrative manuscripts.

## Dependencies and order
I → II → III → IV → V → VI. Parallel research is acceptable, but a later phase cannot upgrade an unverified earlier connection to certainty.

## Deliverables for the next review
1. A corrected migration peoples register with source quotations.
2. A dedicated Vasiṣṭha dossier and a revised Sudās/Viśvāmitra chronology.
3. A location-neutral war coalition matrix.
4. A Harappan receiving-institutions register.
5. An ancient-to-industrial transmission table with direct, indirect and unproven pathways.

The commission will prefer a documented gap to a seamless but invented family tree.
