# The Harappan Receiving Institutions Ledger
*How a newcomer could become a neighbour without erasing the receiving city*

## The principle of reception
A city has its own inhabitants, builders, rites and laws before a foreign house enters its gate. The University's study begins with the receiving institution, not the prestige of the arriving person. Each proposed transfer must specify **what changed**, **who authorized it** and **who retained an independent identity**.

## A model case: a family at the ferry
A household arrives carrying a contract recognized by a frontier court. The Harappan ferry office may accept its guarantee, demand a new witness or refuse the claim. If accepted, the family could become an authorized carrier without acquiring sovereignty over the river. Their children might later inherit an occupation while forgetting the old political name.

This is a **methodological illustration**, not a recovered episode. The commission will not assign it to the Anus, Pūrus or Bhṛgus without a record.

## A model case: a workshop with two traditions
An older Harappan stoneworker trains a migrant metalworker in the city's standards. The migrant shares a furnace practice learned in another house. Their joint workshop develops a technique neither tradition held alone. Centuries later the method might enter an industrial foundry, while both families' political affiliations disappear.

Again, this is an illustrative transmission mechanism. It must not be inserted into the historical event register as fact.

## The independent histories
The Mask Family, Kia alliance, Naga-Raja, Black Lotus Siege, founding of Lothal, Stone Pact and Tendua Crisis retain their established chronology. They are not retitled as episodes of the Ten Kings conflict. Pashupati's court is older than the major Jharwa migrations and cannot be made a late migrant foundation.

## Fieldwork instructions
For each proposed receiving institution, record: its earliest known local name; source and findspot; current custodian; first foreign contact; nature of admission; evidence for change; and the last securely traceable successor. The absence of a record is not evidence of either peaceful assimilation or total destruction.

## The industrial comparison
The Harappan-Lothal Industrial Union has an established inheritance from Harappan stone-engineers, the Mask Family and urbanised Kia artisans. Any additional Bhṛgu, Bharata or other contribution must be identified at the level of a specific method or institution before being included in that history. The Narmada Academic Republic, maritime federations and rail polities likewise cannot be given ancient founders solely through analogy.
