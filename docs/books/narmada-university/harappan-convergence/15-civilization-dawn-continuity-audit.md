# Civilization Dawn: Continuity Audit
*The Narmada University commission's review of the Lothal sequence*

## Scope
This audit compares the proposed late-migration and Ten Kings narratives with the **existing structured chronology**. It does not create a new canonical event. A speculative prehistory cannot displace the primary event graph.

## Fixed chronology and documentary anchors

| Record | Canonical content | Relation to convergence inquiry |
|---|---|---|

## Event graph reconciliation
The **Silvershore War** precedes the founding and Black Lotus Siege in the structured event graph. The **Founding of Lothal** precedes the Black Lotus Siege and Stone Pact. The **Black Lotus Siege** precedes the Stone Pact, and the **Stone Pact** precedes the Tendua Crisis. This is the authoritative partial order; it does not establish an exact day for every event.

The Ten Kings conflict has **no recorded edge** to these events. Neither the river dialogue of Viśvāmitra nor the coalition victory of Sudās may be inserted as the cause of Lothal's founding, the Silvershore debt dispute or the Kia marriage. A relative order within the comparative song cycle does not supply an absolute date in the South of Tethys timeline.

## Existing populations and independent contributions
**Harappan stone-engineers** and the **Mask Family** remain distinct custodians of the Lothal inheritance. **Kia artisans** contribute through the city's established political history. The **Naga Clan** has its own lineage and court traditions, and its recorded conflict with Kavik belongs to Civilization Dawn. **Vedda** oral custodians retain their own chronology and Kashyapa memory.

The presence of Vedda and Indus migrants in the Naga Clan's recorded origins does not demonstrate descent from the Anus, Pūrus or Bharatas. Equally, a Naga artisan sharing a forge practice with a Bhṛgu teacher would demonstrate exchange, not replacement of the clan's ancestry.

## Candidate connections and their disposition

| Proposed link | Finding | Editorial decision |
|---|---|---|
| Sudās founded or ruled Lothal | Conflicts with Kavik's established foundation | Reject |
| Ten Kings battle caused the Silvershore War | No connecting evidence | Do not assert |
| Bhṛgu teachers supplied all Harappan metalworking | Contradicts independent established craft inheritances | Reject |
| A frontier oath practice reached a Lothal ferry | No named dated intermediary | Keep as hypothesis |
| Nārada's witness school founded Lothal record offices | No traceable school or register | Keep as hypothesis |
| Some frontier refugees entered Harappan settlements | Plausible, but no named receiving ward | Keep as hypothesis |
| Naga-Raja was a coalition ruler of the Ten Kings | No identity evidence; separate established history | Reject |
| The Stone Pact drew on earlier frontier treaty customs | Comparative possibility only | Do not assert continuity |
| The industrial Union inherits the Mask Family, Harappan stone-engineers and urbanised Kia | Explicit faction record | Preserve |

## Remaining evidence gaps
The archive does not presently identify a Harappan ward settled by a named coalition group, a local tablet naming Sudās, an independently dated South of Tethys battlefield, or a chain of craft custody from a Bhṛgu house to the Industrial Union. These are documented gaps, not missing scenes to invent.

**Audit result:** The proposed chronicles can coexist with the established Civilization Dawn history **provided the unsupported links remain hypotheses**. The fixed Lothal event graph and independent cultural inheritances remain unchanged.
