# Receiving Houses: How Frontier Peoples Entered the Harappan City-World
*The city as a meeting of institutions rather than a single victorious bloodline*

## The city before the stranger
Harappan settlements possessed their own builders, residents, crafts, civic arrangements, river knowledge and trading traditions. The arrival of a named frontier household may have changed the distribution of labour or law, but it did not erase the people already living there.

The University's task is to trace transfers with named evidence, not to award the foundation of an existing city to a distant battle winner.

## Five doors into city life
### The market
Caravan specialists could become regular contractors, warehouse partners or registered merchants. The Paṇi designation in old adversarial song cannot by itself identify an ethnic merchant colony. Any supposed Paṇi quarter needs an inscription or local testimony.

### The ferry and canal
A Vedda guide, an Anu negotiator or an independent river pilot might be employed at a crossing. The employment would show a practical alliance, not absorption of an entire people.

### The workshop
Craft techniques were taught across households. Fire-keeping methods attributed to Bhṛgu teachers could coexist with older Harappan stonework and Kia artisan knowledge. No lineage owns all smithing merely by preserving one technique.

### The council
A former royal retainer might enter civic service as a witness, collector or clerk. An oath school linked with Viśvāmitra or Nārada could influence procedures, if records survive. The surviving chronicles do not yet identify a named ancient migrant house in Lothal's council.

### Sanctuary and the gate
Refugees and defeated families sometimes obtain security without citizenship, or citizenship without inheriting office. The distinction matters after coalition warfare. A city's willingness to receive refugees does not establish that it conquered their former homeland.

## The Lothal sequence
Lothal's founding, the Stone Pact, the Black Lotus Siege and the Tendua Crisis belong to its established Civilization Dawn history. The university's late-migrant study can be fitted around those anchors only after relative dating is settled.

The Naga-Raja's maritime opposition to Lothal is already part of the local record. It cannot be quietly retold as the reappearance of one of the Ten Kings. The Kia alliance and the Mask Family also retain their established political histories.

## Methods of finding continuity
Strong evidence would be an identifiable personal or house-name across independently dated records, a sequence of comparable contracts with known transmission, or a workshop archive recording its own teachers. Weak evidence is a single repeated word, a resemblance of ritual practice or a broad assumption that all migrants joined a victorious city.

## The city does not end the frontier
Outside Harappan markets, Naga courts, Vedda communities, independent highland rulers and river leagues continued to negotiate on their own terms. Some were trading partners; others were rivals or military opponents. Convergence was partial and reversible.

The result was neither a pure Harappan ancestry nor a collection of untouched enclaves. It was an expanding network of relationships whose different communities retained unequal amounts of political, linguistic and ritual independence.
