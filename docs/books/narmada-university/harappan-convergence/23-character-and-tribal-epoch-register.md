## Event and place reconciliation

- The draft Battle of Ten Kings, Bharata river passage, Viśvāmitra's renunciation and Vasiṣṭha's recitals are assigned to migrations. The local battlefield and relationship to later Hastinapura remain open.
- The draft Yadu foundation event (event_yadu_foundation_north_dwarka) and Bharata settlement event (event_bharata_settlement_hastinapura) are Civilization Dawn records. North Dwarka's regional hegemony precedes Lothal's rise, not necessarily its founding. No exact date or causal edge to Kavik's founding is established. The settlement and place records currently describe only Age of Machinery states; their earlier histories are supplied by these draft events and chapters 17–18.
- The draft event_bhrgu_teaching_enters_vedda_guilds is undated; the Chandraketugarh blood feud is assigned to Age of Machinery. Later custodians, not necessarily the original ṛṣis, enter the guilds. Garudasura moss is a guild name for Garudasaur blood material, distinct from Blue Scholar's Moss and Toxic Red Spore-Moss flora.
- The draft event_agasthya_yaksha_southern_exchange is assigned to Age of Machinery. It does not establish an exact itinerary or personal meeting with Kubera.

## Editorial boundary

This register documents the current archive and adopted narrative. Future structured changes may add early settlement states and supported chronological relationships, and synchronize the adopted Nārada, Urva and Aurva epoch assignments. Exact dates and the timing of the guild transmission event remain open. No database changes or canon promotions are made by this manuscript.
