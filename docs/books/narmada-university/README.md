**Continuity reference:** [Asura Character–Event Concordance](character-event-concordance.md) links the characters to recorded events, identifies conflicting first-appearance epochs and unresolved locations, and distinguishes hidden witnesses from event participants.

## Continuity rules
- Sarishne is Rakshasa; Gagdji is Oni; contact with Asura history is not Asura descent.
- Varuṇa is celestial deity and Keeper of Ṛta, bearing the in-universe title Asura Mahat; Vairochana is an Elder Asura.
- No major epic protagonists are introduced as inhabitants of South of Tethys.
- Asura Loka is not a verified geographical homeland.
- Rahu and Ketu are **mathematical eclipse nodes**; consciousness is unproven. They are not identified with living Asura planets.
- Manjalaya's death chronology remains provisional; Vaishura remains one deceased individual.
- No character JSON, game schema, event participant, location or gameplay modifications are made by these manuscripts.

## Status
Draft university manuscripts for editorial review. The original approved character dossiers remain intact pending a separate dependency and taxonomy migration.

## New political-history series (draft)
[From Civilization Dawn to the Nations of Machinery](nine-traditions/README.md) reconstructs the missing state-building centuries: dynasties and confederacies, commercial leagues, ethnic movements, constitutional struggles and the transitions into eight existing Age of Machinery polities. The nine traditions' local testimony dossiers are **supporting source narratives**, not a substitute for political history. All new treaties, revolutions and intermediate polities are editorial proposals; no structured canon is changed.


### Proposed elapsed chronology and sky-flight continuity
[The Two Thousand and Two Hundred Years](nine-traditions/13-two-thousand-two-hundred-years.md) sets a **provisional** 2,000–2,400-year Civilization Dawn → early Machinery interval (working anchor: 2,200 years), with no general Bronze Age collapse. It preserves Rathak's **already-operating whale blimps in Civilization Dawn** and instead traces the later scale-up of sky-island surveys, rare minerals, airship logistics, engines and industrial states. The 1920s-like Current Era may postdate the threshold of Machinery. This is not an amendment to canonical era dates.


## Character epoch correction
All nineteen individuals in the Nine Houses register are mortal political actors of the **Early Age of Machinery**, active around or after the proposed Year 2,200 threshold. They personally participate in industrial nation-building, constitutional settlements and ethnic or labour movements. Earlier institutions and customs belong to unnamed predecessors, not these individuals. Any named movement or settlement involving them belongs to Early Machinery; earlier phases describe its institutional antecedents only. See [the character register](nine-traditions/00-chronicle-of-nine-houses.md). Exact lifetimes remain unassigned; the proposed elapsed chronology and existing Dawn characters are unchanged.
