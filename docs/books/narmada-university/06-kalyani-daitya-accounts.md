# Kalyani and the Daitya Accounts

*Studies in Ancient Testimony, Volume VI*  
*Narmada University — Collegium of Comparative Lineages*

## Preface
The name Kalyani survives in a short but important dossier. She is classified as Daitya. The scarcity of information should be acknowledged rather than concealed by elaborate inventions.

## I. The Distinct Name
Daitya, Danava and Asura are not interchangeable labels in the University's present catalogues. Their relations may be argued by later scholars, but a single tradition cannot erase the distinctions preserved in the surviving records.

## II. Kalyani
The approved testimony establishes Kalyani's Daitya identity and her friendship with Vusajc Karthakur. Her epoch, location, mortality, abilities and political affiliations remain unknown. Any purported royal genealogy would exceed the evidence.

## III. Vusajc Karthakur
The friend is named, but their species is not. Friendship is neither proof of common descent nor evidence of membership in a particular court.

## IV. The Problem of Sparse Sources
Silence in the archive may indicate lost documents, an unwritten tradition or a life lived beyond the chroniclers' interest. It does not license the invention of a biography. Future discoveries may alter the classification, but until then the record must retain its uncertainties.

## Closing Judgment
A short, accurate dossier is preferable to a splendid false chronicle. Kalyani remains Daitya; Vusajc remains unclassified.
