### 🦎 The "Nagaraptor vs. Naga" Debate: Canon Distinction & In-Universe Rumors

To handle this cleanly in the worldbuilding and database schema, here is the official distinction alongside the in-universe rumor that exists among scholars and storytellers:

---

#### 1. The Canon Biological Reality (Two Unrelated Lineages)
* **Nagaraptors (`Proto-Jambhu Dromaeosaur`):** Native diapsid theropod dinosaurids of the **Taj caste**. They are feathered, social raptors who form symbiotic partnerships with burrowing tortoises (`Geohelus domus`) to construct multi-level earthen **Taj Nest-Homes**. They belong to the ancient diapsid "Scale" alliance from the *Fang vs. Scale Wars*.
* **The Naga Clan / Naga-Kin:** Human Vedda/Indus migrants who settled the Deccan Plateau and Saraswati Delta. They are spiritual and biological descendants of **Rakshak Takshak** (the shape-shifting stone serpent with obsidian wings). They practice serpent-cult rites, metallurgy, and political espionage.

---

#### 2. The In-Universe Rumor: "The Naga-Raptor Fallacy"
In **Epoch 4 (The Age of Machinery)**, scholars at Narmada University stumbled across ancient Proto-Brahmi glyphs containing the root word *"Nāga"* (meaning *scale/serpent/river-ruler*):

* **Professor Onko’s Fallacy:** Onko published a controversial bestiary paper asserting that the human Naga clan were actually "devolved bipedal raptors" who lost their feathers and grew smooth scales over millions of years, pointing to the Taj-caste's scale-patterned ceremonial body paint as proof.
* **Dr. Digha Jani’s Correction:** Field historian Digha Jani disproved this, showing that *"Nāga"* was simply an ancient descriptor used by Jharwa cave-painters for **any intelligent, scale-bearing river-dweller**. 
* **The Delta Folk-Rumor:** Among sailors and delta merchants at Lothal, a popular superstition persists that if a Nagaraptor lives for a hundred years in a sacred stepwell, its feathers turn to obsidian scales, allowing it to shape-shift into a human **Naga Infiltrator**.
